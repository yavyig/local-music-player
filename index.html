Support

Help with your music and video library, subtitles, playback and file transfers in Local Player Offline 1.2 for iPhone and iPad.

Get in touch

yavuzalper@me.com

Include your device model, iOS version, app version and what happened. You can find the app version in Settings. Please do not send passwords or files you do not have permission to share.

Add your music and videos

  1. Open the app and tap the add button in Library.
  2. Choose audio files, video files or a folder from Files, iCloud Drive or connected storage.
  3. Keep the app open while the import finishes. Imported files are copied into the app and appear in Library and Folders.

You can also add files in Files → On My iPhone → Local Player Offline, or use Finder file sharing on a Mac. On an iPad, use On My iPad. Files stored with a cloud provider must be downloaded and available locally for offline playback.

Link a folder without copying

Open Settings → Linked Folders → Link Folder… and select a folder in Files. The app remembers access to that folder and indexes supported media in its original location. A folder from another app can be linked when that app exposes it through Files and allows folder access.

Linked files must remain available locally. If a provider is unavailable, the folder is moved, or access changes, make the folder available in Files and try refreshing the library or linking it again. Unlinking a folder removes its link from the player without deleting the original files. Manage those originals in Files or their provider app.

Watch video or listen in the background

Videos play in the Now Playing artwork area. Rotate your device to landscape for fullscreen playback, or use the fullscreen control. Audio can continue when you leave the app or lock your device. Use the audio-only control when you want to listen without displaying video.

Open Video Settings using the gear button. Choose audio or video tracks when the file contains alternatives, adjust playback speed, choose Fit or Fill, or change audio timing and channel output. Available track choices depend on the file. Fill may crop the picture.

Subtitles and timing

The player supports embedded subtitles and compatible subtitle files beside a movie. Use the same base filename, such as Movie.mkv and Movie.srt; a language suffix such as Movie.en.srt is also supported. Common formats include SRT, VTT, ASS/SSA and TTML. VobSub subtitles require both matching IDX and SUB files.

In Video Settings, choose a subtitle track or Off, change subtitle size, or use Add Subtitle Files… to import subtitles from Files. Select both IDX and SUB files together for VobSub. Imported subtitles are stored in the app; the original movie folder is not modified.

Subtitle Appearance offers eight styles for plain-text subtitles, including white or yellow text on black, outlines, soft shadows and a translucent background. ASS/SSA subtitles may use their own authored styles; image-based subtitles keep their original appearance. Size and appearance are saved for that video. Changing appearance briefly reloads the video while keeping your playback position.

Adjust Subtitle Delay with the plus and minus buttons or enter a signed value in seconds. Positive values show subtitles later; negative values show them earlier. Reset returns the delay to zero. Subtitle settings are saved for that movie. Subtitle compatibility depends on the file contents and decoder support.

Supported formats and sound

Common audio formats include FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3 and AAC. DSF and uncompressed DFF files are converted from DSD to PCM for playback. Common video containers include MP4, MOV, M4V, AVI, MKV and WebM. Open Settings → Supported Formats for the full extension list. A recognized extension does not guarantee that every codec or file variant will play.

If a file will not play, confirm that it is fully downloaded, still accessible and not protected by a subscription service or DRM. Try another local file. The equalizer and Sound Normalizer apply to compatible audio files; video has its own playback controls. Output quality depends on your device and connected audio equipment.

Find, share and organize files

Use Search to find your media. Open a file’s options and choose Show in Folder to jump to its original location; the app briefly highlights it. Use the share icon in Now Playing to send the current audio or video file through the iOS share sheet. Only share files you have permission to distribute.

Create playlists in the Playlists tab and add audio or video from your library. You can also import M3U/M3U8 playlists with your collection. Imported playlists need access to the media files they reference.

Lyrics and album artwork

Place an LRC file with the same base filename beside a song for synchronized lyrics, or use a matching TXT file for plain lyrics. The app also reads supported embedded lyrics. It does not download lyrics from the internet.

If you open the lyrics view and no lyrics are available, a brief notice appears for ten seconds, then the album artwork returns. The lyrics view remains selected so the next song can show its available lyrics automatically.

Choose an app icon

Open Settings → App Icon to choose from ten music and media styles. If iOS is temporarily unable to change the icon, keep the app in the foreground and try again in a moment.

Transfer over Wi-Fi

Connect both devices to the same trusted local network. In the app, open Settings → Wi-Fi Transfer, turn transfer on, and enter the displayed address in a browser on your other device. Include http:// and the port number. Keep the app open during transfer.

The transfer page can browse, upload, download and manage files in the app’s own library folder. It does not expose linked folders from other apps. If the page does not open, allow Local Network access in iOS Settings, check that both devices are on the same network, and try a network without guest-device isolation. Turn Wi-Fi Transfer off when you finish.

Keep a copy of your collection

Delete from Device removes an imported file from the app’s local storage. Linked originals are managed in Files or their provider app. Deleting the app removes its local library, imported subtitles and settings; files in linked folders remain with their original provider. Keep a separate copy of important media and playlists before deleting files or uninstalling.
