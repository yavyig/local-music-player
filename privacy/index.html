Privacy policy

Local Player Offline is a music and video player for files you supply. It is developed by Yavuz Yigitoglu and is designed to play and organize your media on your device. This policy covers version 1.2.

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Data stays on your device

The app does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer through an analytics, advertising or tracking service. It does not sell personal information or track you across apps or websites.

Imported audio and video files, file metadata, artwork, lyrics, imported subtitles, playlists, favorites, play counts, playback history and settings are stored locally to provide the player’s features. Linked-folder access records and movie-specific playback and subtitle settings are also stored on your device. Smart EQ and loudness analysis for compatible audio files run on the device. The app does not upload your library to a developer-operated server or download lyrics or subtitles from an online service.

Files you choose to import, link or share

When you import media or subtitle files, the app reads the files and folders you choose and copies the imported files into its own storage. Imported subtitles do not change the movie’s original folder. If you select files from iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles the download under its own terms and privacy policy.

Linking a folder is different from importing it. The app saves access to the folder you select and reads supported media, artwork, lyrics and subtitle companions in their original location. It does not copy the folder into the app or delete its original files when you unlink it. Access depends on the permissions and availability provided by Files and the original provider. Linked files must be downloaded and available locally for offline playback.

If you use the system share sheet, the destination app or service receives the files you choose to share. Copies you export or share remain under your control and the recipient’s policies.

Optional local Wi-Fi transfers

Wi-Fi Transfer is optional and requires local network permission. While it is enabled and the app is open, the app runs a local web server so another device can browse, upload, download and manage files in the app’s own library folder using the displayed address. Linked folders from other apps are not exposed through this transfer page.

Transfers take place between your devices rather than through a developer-operated cloud service. The local transfer page uses HTTP. Use a trusted private network, share the address only with people you trust, and turn Wi-Fi Transfer off when you finish. Other devices that can reach that address may access the transfer page while it is enabled.

Device permissions and Apple services

The app uses file access to import your selected media and subtitles or read folders you choose to link. It uses local network access when you enable Wi-Fi Transfer. You can manage permissions in iOS Settings and remove folder links in Settings → Linked Folders inside the app.

Features provided by the operating system, such as iCloud device backups, AirPlay, Bluetooth, App Store purchases, ratings and optional diagnostic sharing, are subject to your device settings and the relevant provider’s policies. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

You can remove imported local media with Delete from Device or through Files. Linked originals are managed in Files or their provider app; removing a folder link does not delete those originals. Deleting the app removes its locally stored library, imported subtitles, playlists, folder-access records and settings. Offloading an app can retain its documents and data. Device backups, exported copies and files held by other providers may remain until you remove them separately.

Keep a separate copy of media you want to retain before deleting files or uninstalling the app.

Contacting support

If you email yavuzalper@me.com, your email address and the information you include are received through the email service and used to answer your request and investigate the issue. This information is provided by you when you contact support; it is not collected automatically by the app.

Do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or files you do not have permission to share. You can use the same email address to ask about or request deletion of information you have sent to support.

This support website

This website does not include an advertising service, an analytics tracker or a contact form. Visiting it sends ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser information, to the hosting infrastructure needed to deliver the page. The site is hosted using GitHub Pages; the hosting service’s practices are described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and questions

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
